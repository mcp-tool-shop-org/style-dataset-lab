# Portlight Ships — Visual Constitution

This constitution governs all vessel plates, hull profiles, and maritime studies for `portlight-ships`. Every candidate must pass R1–R10 before inclusion in any CC-BY-4.0 dataset snapshot.

## Core Principles

1. **R1 — Distinct Hull & Rig Silhouette**: Each of the 14 canonical hull classes (`sloop`, `cog`, `galley`, `fluyt`, `schooner`, `brigantine`, `xebec`, `caravel`, `brig`, `barque`, `carrack`, `junk`, `frigate`, `galleon`) must be immediately distinguishable in silhouette by hull sheer, stem/sternpost rake, and mast/sail plan.
2. **R2 — Readable Deck & Maritime Scale Cues**: Bulwark rail heights, gunport intervals, hatch coamings, and ship's boat chocks must anchor the vessel's tonnage and tier (`coastal-sloop` through `first-rate`).
3. **R3 — Honest Timber, Canvas, and Cordage Detail**: Clinker or carvel strakes, standing shrouds, deadeyes, and reef points must reflect real shipwright joinery rather than noisy surface greeble.
4. **R4 — Rigging & Sail-Plan Clarity**: Square yards, lateen antennal spars, gaff booms, or battened lug sails must attach cleanly to masts and stays.
5. **R5 — Tradition-Authentic Hull Form**: Every hull must honour its shipwright tradition (`cold-coast`, `sunward-sea`, `crown-naval`, `longshore`, `far-trade`).
6. **R6 — Material Separation**: Pitch-sealed oak, oiled pine spars, weathered flax/hemp canvas, and forged iron or verdigris copper must separate cleanly in value and roughness.
7. **R7 — Controlled Maritime Palette**: Strictly grounded in natural timber, tar, tallow ochre, tanbark, and mineral pigments; no neon or modern synthetic finishes.
8. **R8 — Earned Sea-Salt & Service Weathering**: Waterline fouling, salt spray on the bow, and sun-faded canvas follow physical exposure and damage-state tier.
9. **R9 — Unclipped Masthead-to-Waterline Framing**: Complete vessel framing from jibboom/bowsprit tip to taffrail and waterline to maintruck.
10. **R10 — Seaworthy Hydrostatic & Rig Balance**: Coherent waterline trim and physically plausible center of effort across the rig.
