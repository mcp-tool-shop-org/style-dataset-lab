## 2. The decisions this session made

### 2a. The register is asset-level, and NO-LoRA is a positive declaration

Ruling 10b's operative clause for the lane is *"no fixture may leave the section implicit
again."* A field that can be *omitted* cannot carry that. So `style.lora` requires a
`declared` discriminator with exactly two legal values, and:

- a `style` block with no `lora` key is **refused**
- `declared: "none"` **with** a `card` is **refused** (self-contradiction, not silently
  resolved either way)
- `declared: "card"` with no card name is **refused**
- `weight` must be absent or `0` under `"none"` — which is how a profile's `lora-w 0.0`
  expresses it mechanically

The `style` block itself stays optional, because refusing manifests without it would refuse
the current training input. When absent, records carry `provenance.style: null` — a value
**distinguishable from** a ruled no-LoRA register — and the ingest emits a gap notice.

The lane does **not** model the class taxonomy. facet's `docs/style-registers.md` splits
classes by register (creature-companion differs from beast because its register is tamer,
not menacing); the lane carries whatever terms each asset declares, which is what makes a
training pool filterable by register at all.

> One forward note, flagged not acted on: that same doc names sdlab as the natural home for
> the LoRA card registry ("one page per card… sdlab is its natural home — training is
> sdlab's lane"). That is a separate deliverable, not started, and not in this session's
> scope. `style.lora.card` is deliberately a **live card name string**, not a foreign key —
> so a registry can be built later without a schema change.

### 2b. The tone transform IS carried — declared, contained, hashed, materialized

**The question posed to this lane was whether a declared tone-transform field should carry
the harmonization operands. Decision: yes.**

The reason is not completeness. It is that this lane *measures colour* — the palette gate,
the per-class shares, and the pre-registered ΔE experiment (baseline 17.97 / 17.56) all
produce numbers that sit downstream of any upstream tone map. Ruling 23f adopted
harmonization for projection; the renders that reach the lane therefore derive from
harmonized sources. Pooling harmonized-source and raw-source assets is a legitimate
choice, but making it unknowingly is not, and neither is comparing a ΔE from one against a
ΔE from the other. An undeclared transform makes that comparison silently invalid.

What the lane checks — and only this:

- `reference` names a render **this manifest declares**. A reference view absent from the
  export is dangling provenance, and that is structurally checkable.
- the `operands` path is contained inside the export, parses as a JSON object, and is
  hashed.

What the lane does **not** check, deliberately and in the contract's own words: that the
transform was applied, that the operands are correct, or that the arithmetic is reversible.
Same declared-weak boundary as the `json` encoding. sdlab records the asset's claims; it
does not verify semantics it does not own.

One deviation from the surrounding convention, made on purpose: the operands file is
**materialized** into `assets/<id>/`, where the mesh, atlas and texture channels beside it
are hashed in place. It is small, and it is the only record of what the transform did to
these colours — leaving it as a pointer would make the lane's audit trail depend on the
facet export tree surviving. That dependency already exists for the galleon's owner channel
and did not need a second instance.

Per-render `tone_transform: false` opts out. The ingest **resolves** the boolean once and
writes the answer (plus `reference_is_self`, true for the reference view whose transfer
toward itself is identity) into every record, so nothing downstream re-derives a default.

### 2c. Generation frame and seed are per-image, and `frame` is a closed enum

Both phenomena are per-image, so both fields are per-render.

`frame` is closed to `"full"` | `"crop"` for the same reason `facing` is a derived
vocabulary: it is the axis the register-drift phenomenon was measured on, and a free-text
field where one asset writes `"bust"` and another `"head-crop"` cannot be grouped. Finer
naming rides in `frame_detail`, which is the asset's business.

`seed` accepts a safe integer **or a digit-string** — a seed too large for an exact JSON
number silently loses its low bits, and a seed that cannot be grouped on exactly is useless
for the purpose it is here for.

`reroll_of` is **not** required to resolve within the manifest: superseded artifacts are
commonly retained upstream rather than exported. It marks that a render is a re-roll, which
is itself the marker that a seed resisted.

**~~One correction to the brief~~ — WITHDRAWN 2026-08-07, ruled in E11 addenda 3.**

This receipt originally claimed the brief's "four stems" was a misreading of
`E12-twin-prompts.json`'s seed×view map coverage, and that the ruled figure was three. **That
correction was itself wrong**, and the facet record settled it: *both numbers live in the
record, on different bases.* Ruling 20a counts **three** stems at handoff 10 (unnamed,
compound, split); Ruling 21c then records **"the struts' fourth stem"** at handoff 11, where
view 4 at 770700 resists two different terms. The brief's four and this receipt's three are
the same facts counted from different handoffs.

Nothing downstream turns on it — per-image seed provenance is warranted under either count,
which is why the schema is unaffected. Recorded because a correction that is itself wrong is
worse than no correction: it would have propagated as the lane's authority on a fact the
lane does not own.

## 5. The standing errand — status corrected

**The errand:** `identity.subject_name` must be in the data before any training split
exists (galleon/w3, plus the dragon on ingest).

**Corrected finding, measured this session.** The prior framing held that the staged
`w3_warrior` (8 records) and `w3_warrior_dense` (26 records) read as two subject families
of one subject and therefore leak. That is **not currently true, and the reason matters**:
the staged W3's 8 records were retired by curation, carrying
`judgment.status: "rejected"` with the supersession reason. `createSplit` builds only from
a snapshot's included entries, and `evaluateEligibility` gates inclusion on judgment status
— so the retired generation cannot reach a snapshot, and therefore cannot reach a split.
**The staged-W3 leak is closed at the snapshot boundary.**

**What is genuinely open** is the forward case, and it is not closed by anything above:

1. **All 54 live records resolve their subject family by *guessing*.** With no
   `identity.subject_name`, `lib/split.js` falls back to `normalizeIdStem`, and
   `familyGuessed` fires for 100% of asset records. A guessed family is correct only by
   luck of the id scheme.
2. **The dragon will land as a third guessed family**, and if any second dragon export ever
   arrives under a different asset id — a re-export, a crop set exported separately, a
   post-Gate-1 revision — the two strip to different stems and read as two subjects.
3. **Retiring by curation does not generalise.** It worked here because a whole generation
   was superseded at once. Two *concurrent* live exports of one subject have no such
   mechanism, and that is exactly what a dense set plus a later revision looks like.

**The fix is unchanged and is export-side:** facet re-emits with `identity.subject_name`
(`galleon`, `w3`, and `dragon` on ingest), then the lane re-ingests. The schema has accepted
the field since 1.1.0; nothing in sdlab blocks it. **The window is still open — no split
exists.** Once one does, the fix costs re-cutting every split built without it.

## 9. Schema 1.3.0 — the defect 1.2.0 shipped with **[AMENDED 2026-08-07]**

**Found by facet's endorsement, not by this lane.** E11 addenda 3 recorded the dragon's
export requirements against 1.2.0 and specified:

> `renders[].generation`: **absent on every emit render, deliberately** — emit renders are
> deterministic derivations of the accepted asset (Ruling 2), not generated images; an
> optional block that does not apply is left out, not filled in.

Under 1.2.0 that manifest would have produced `ASSET_GENERATION_PROVENANCE_ABSENT` — a
**gap** notice, whose message says closing it is export-side work — on every one of the
dragon's renders. The export would have been correct by ruling and the lane would have
reported it as needing a fix.

That is worse than a cosmetic wrong. A notice channel that cries wolf on correct data stops
being read, and it would have taken the other three notices' credibility with it — including
`ASSET_SUBJECT_NAME_ABSENT`, which is the standing errand's only automated alarm.

**The fix follows the lane's own law.** `style.lora.declared` exists because silence and
declaration are different facts and the lane refuses to collapse them. The same principle
applies here, so:

```jsonc
"asset": {
  "render_derivation": { "kind": "emit", "generated": false, "record": "E11 Ruling 2" }
}
```

| `render_derivation` | per-render `generation` | notice |
|---|---|---|
| `generated: false` | **refused** as a category error | `ASSET_RENDERS_ARE_DERIVATIONS` (info) |
| `generated: true` | validated as in 1.2.0 | gap when absent |
| absent | validated as in 1.2.0 | gap when absent — 1.2.0 behaviour preserved exactly |

Two deliberate choices inside that:

- **The refusal under `generated: false` is strict on purpose.** A deterministic derivation
  has no seed of its own; declaring one attributes a seed to an image that no seed produced.
  The twins' generating provenance is real and belongs to those twins — it lives in their
  sidecars facet-side, exactly as the addendum says.
- **An undeclared manifest still gets the gap.** Saying nothing is not the same as saying
  "these are derivations." The gap notice now names its own remedy, so an export that *is*
  emit-derived learns how to say so.

Records carry `provenance.render_derivation` beside `provenance.generation` — which is what
makes the two kinds of `null` distinguishable. A curator filtering for unknown-seed records
must not sweep up derivations that never had one.

**Why 1.3.0 rather than amending 1.2.0.** 1.2.0 is endorsed by name in facet's committed
record. Changing what that version means after another repo has ruled against it would break
the reference. The lane's own rule about changed exports — *a changed export is a new truth,
not an overwrite* — applies to its own contract versions too.

**Acceptance test.** `tests/lib-asset/asset-style-provenance.test.js` now carries **THE
DRAGON SHAPE** — a manifest built to the addendum's exact requirements (register + LoRA
none · tone_transform toward the reference emit-render id with materialized operands ·
`render_derivation` emit/false · no generation blocks · `identity.subject_name`) asserted to
ingest with **zero gap notices**. If the lane ever drifts from what facet was told it
accepts, that test fails. **[MEASURED — passing]**

## 10. The twin set: recommendation **[AMENDED 2026-08-07]**

**The question**, from E11 addenda 3 and put to this lane: whether the E12/E13 twin set also
enters the lane as a declared generated-image ingest, now fully declarable under 1.2.0. The
default is emit-renders-only, per E11 Ruling 2. It is decided at the ingest paste, not here.

**Recommendation: hold the default. Emit renders only — and the twins ride as declared
conditioning pairs, not as a second asset.**

The reasoning is structural, not conservative:

1. **They already have a home in the schema.** `renders[].pair` declares a clay↔styled twin
   *on the emit render it corresponds to*. That is lossless linkage — path plus hash, carried
   from day one precisely so the ~500–1,000-pair threshold can activate retroactively. A
   separate asset ingest would build a second, parallel representation of a relationship the
   contract already models better.
2. **A separate ingest reopens the leakage the standing errand exists to close.** Twins of
   the dragon are the *same subject*. Ingested as their own asset they either declare a
   different `identity.subject_name` — two families, one subject, which is precisely the
   train/test condition §5 is about — or they declare the same one, in which case they are
   one family carrying two derivation classes, and `renders[].pair` expresses that without
   the ambiguity.
3. **It would put per-view-inconsistent images into a pool whose entire premise is
   view-consistency by construction.** The twins carry the owner-seam class the emit path
   exists to avoid — the same ΔE 17.97 baseline this lane pre-registered against. Mixing
   them dilutes the one property that makes the asset lane worth having.
4. **Much of the twin set is superseded or rejected.** Ruling 20b made it the E12
   *measurement record* — retained in full, superseded as production artifacts. Ruling 24b's
   crop twins: *"nothing they painted is adopted."* `docs/style-registers.md`'s standing law
   is that nothing trains on rejected artifacts. Admitting the set wholesale would need a
   per-render curation pass to re-separate what a ruling already separated.

**The named condition under which this re-opens** — so the default is a decision, not an
inertia: the conditioning-pair threshold. At ~500–1,000 accumulated pairs the design doc
already anticipates a `conditioning-pair` package type. At that point the twins become
training material *as pairs*, and the question becomes live in a different form.

**What that re-open would need, recorded now so it is not rediscovered:** `renders[].pair`
currently carries a path only. Twins entering as training material would want their seed and
stem on the pair, which means a richer pair form (`{path, generation}`) — a small additive
minor. **Deliberately not built this session**: it is speculative until the threshold is in
sight, and the twins' generation provenance lives in facet's sidecars until then, exactly as
the addendum states.

**This recommendation is reversible at the paste and costs nothing to reverse** — 1.2.0/1.3.0
make the twins fully declarable either way. Nothing here forecloses the Director's call.
