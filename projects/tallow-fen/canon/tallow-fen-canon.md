# The Tallow Fen — bestiary canon (test run)

> Fresh, original canon authored 2026-06-09 to test the non-anime Qwen pipeline. Not tied to any
> existing game. Canon = training data for the style model: every detail is concrete and depictable.

## Premise
**The Tallow Fen** is a sunless peat-bog frontier where the drowned dead and rendered animal fat
congeal into slow, grieving things. Its creatures are "mires" — bodies of wet peat, dripping tallow,
and the stained bones of what they swallowed.

## Art direction (the style canon)
- **Rendering:** hand-painted oil-and-ink; visible loaded brushwork; soft **charcoal outlines**
  (never crisp vector); matte scumbled surfaces; canvas grain shows.
- **Master palette:** peat `#2b2620` · bog-green `#4a5535` · tallow-gold `#c8a24a` ·
  bone-ash `#d9d2be` · dried-rust `#6e2b22`.
- **Lighting:** one warm tallow/lantern source in cold sunless gloom — chiaroscuro, deep shadow,
  a thin warm rim. No flat even light.
- **Silhouette language:** heavy, sagging, asymmetric organic masses; drooping verticals; reads as
  one clotted shape at 64px. No spiky armored points.
- **Materials:** *peat-flesh* (wet matted fibrous bog-body) · *tallow* (translucent amber wax that
  drips and sets) · *fen-bone* (stained yellow-brown bone, antler, driftwood).

| THIS | NOT THAT |
|---|---|
| hand-painted oil-and-ink brushwork | clean vector / cel-shading |
| muted earthen palette | saturated neon |
| sagging organic mass | spiky anime armor silhouette |
| one warm lantern in cold gloom | flat even lighting |
| grim folkloric, grounded | cute mascot / chibi |

## Creatures
- **Tallow Hound** — *scavenger/pack hunter.* Low-slung emaciated quadruped, dog-sized, hunched,
  drooping head. *64px:* a starved four-legged sag with a heavy drooping head and rib ridges.
  *Palette:* `#2b2620`, `#c8a24a`, `#d9d2be`. *Materials:* matted peat-flesh hide (no fur), tallow,
  exposed fen-bone ribs. *Features:* solid **tallow drips hanging frozen from its eye-sockets**; bare
  yellowed rib-bones; no fur. *Forbidden:* a clean glowing-eyed wolf.
- **Mire Widow** — *ambush/lure.* Tall (~2.5m) boneless biped drooping forward, long sagging arms
  trailing into the bog. *64px:* a tall narrow drooping vertical with trailing arms, a hunched
  candle-figure. *Palette:* `#4a5535`, `#2b2620`, `#c8a24a`. *Materials:* peat-flesh draped like wet
  rags, a tallow lantern-organ, fen-bone fingers. *Features:* a **translucent amber tallow "lantern"
  glowing inside its hollow chest** (the lure); long boneless arms ending in fen-bone claws;
  **veiled, faceless drooping head**. *Forbidden:* a human face / an elegant witch.
- **Bog Tithe** — *slow heavy.* Wide squat amorphous mound, human-wide, bulbous, no clear limbs,
  drags itself. *64px:* a broad heavy lump with a sagging top, a clotted boulder-mass. *Palette:*
  `#2b2620`, `#6e2b22`, `#d9d2be`. *Materials:* compacted peat-flesh studded with fen-bone, rust-red
  runoff. *Features:* **protruding stained bones and antlers of swallowed things**; rust-red water
  weeping from its seams; one sunken tallow eye. *Forbidden:* a clean-geometry rock golem.

## Global must-not
anime / chibi / cel-shaded · a human in a costume · photoreal or 3D-render · modern gear (metal
plate, guns, tech).
