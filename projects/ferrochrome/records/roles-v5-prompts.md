# Role set v5 — pinned prompts (2026-08-22)

Generated by `scratchpad/build_v5.py`; record and submitted batch come from the same strings.

## What changed from v4

1. **The face is eye-study-2 variant B** — fully human eyes (sclera, iris fibres, limbal ring, wet
   pupil, catchlight, soft lids) with **no eyelashes and no eyebrows**, on a smooth composite brow.
   Director's ruling: the v4 lens optics read frightening on the faction that is supposed to be
   sympathetic.
2. **HEAD_WEAR is now a separate, mandatory block.** This is the load-bearing change and the thing
   that makes human eyes safe: panel seams across crown/temple/jaw, the paler replacement cheek
   panel, scratches, a sealed hairline crack, and machined vertebrae with cable looms at the neck.
   **The eyes no longer carry the manufactured read — this block does.** Drop it and the face goes
   human.
3. **CHEST stated positively as blank** — "a single unbroken panel, no lens, no aperture, no port,
   no housing." v4's companion and security both grew a camera lens on exposed chest plating.
4. **Framing hardened** — "wide shot, the entire figure from the top of the head to both feet flat
   on the floor inside the frame," plus `cropped, portrait crop, cut off legs, cut off feet` in the
   negative. Only 1 of 6 v4 images was genuinely head-to-feet.
5. **Negative rebuilt around the failures actually observed** — hardware-looking eyes, hair on the
   faceplate, the chest lens, cropping. The eye-study-2 lesson: aim the negative at the failure you
   got, not the one you were guarding against last round.

## Prediction under test

The chest lens should be gone even without the CHEST clause, because the lens vocabulary that
leaked onto the torso is no longer in the prompt at all. Both fixes ship together here, so a clean
result confirms the outcome but not the mechanism.

## Findings

All six looked at full-size. Outputs in `outputs/candidates/roles-v5/`. 6 submitted, 6 completed,
0 failed.

### ✅ The face works across the whole set

Every role carries the same warm human-eyed face — sclera, iris, catchlight, soft lids, no lashes,
no brows, kind gaze — on a composite head that still reads as built. ROLE-001 holds: the face is
constant, the chassis carries the role. Nothing in the set reads frightening.

### ✅ The chest lens is GONE — including on the worst case

**Security is the proof.** Its chest plate is fully exposed through an open duty coat, which is
exactly the configuration that produced a large camera lens in v4, and the plate is now blank and
unbroken. Companion, the other v4 failure, is also clean.

Both fixes shipped together — the lens vocabulary left the prompt *and* the blank-chest clause was
added — so this confirms the **outcome** but not the **mechanism**. The prediction that removing
the lens language alone would have been sufficient remains untested. Don't record it as proven.

### ✅ Alpha correct out of the graph again

Border ring 0–11% opaque across all six, no repair needed. Second consecutive clean batch since
`InvertMask` went in.

### ⚠ Framing: fixed on 4 of 6, still failing on 2

| Full figure with both feet | Cropped |
|---|---|
| companion, security, maintenance, agricultural | **warehouse**, **medical** |

Up from 1 of 6 in v4, so the wide-shot phrasing and the `cut off legs / cut off feet` negatives are
working — but not reliably. Both failures are mid-thigh crops. Worth noting that the two failures
are also the two bulkiest silhouettes in the set, which may be the model trading framing for
subject size.

### ⚠ Open question the Director has not ruled on: the face demographic

All six read young and somewhat feminine, and they are consistent about it — which is *correct* for
ROLE-001, since one face is supposed to be the species marker. But it is one demographic for every
outcast in the game, and it was chosen by the seed rather than by decision. This should be a
deliberate call before the dataset is built, not a property inherited from `770422`.
