# Role set v4 — pinned prompts (2026-08-22)

Generated by `scratchpad/build_v4.py`; the record and the submitted batch come from the same

## What changed from v3

1. **Eyes** — variant **C** from `eye-study-prompts.md`, Director-selected: a counterbored two-tier
   socket, bezel rings carrying grime and wear, segmented iris ring, black pupil, unlit. Plus the
   explicit **no lashes / no brows / no lid crease / no sclera** clause, which is what actually
   killed the v3 human-eye read.
2. **Ear named explicitly** — "a plain moulded ear on each side of the head, closed and
   featureless." Insurance against the third-optic failure that eye-study A and D produced, where
   the bezel-ring motif spread to the next round feature on the head. Variant C did not show it,
   but the clause is free.
3. **Replacement panel named positively** — "a *paler* off-white, slightly brighter than the shell
   around it," never "a slightly different off-white." Describing it by difference rendered a flat
   black rectangle in three of four eye studies.
4. **`InvertMask` in the graph** — RMBG's MASK output is background-positive. Without this every
   output is an android-shaped hole. See HANDOFF.md.
5. **Torso housing stated positively** — law #5, the nude-body attractor, countered in the prompt
   rather than only in the negative.

## Findings

All six looked at full-size before anything was written here. Outputs in
`outputs/candidates/roles-v4/`. 6 submitted, 6 completed, 0 failed.

### ✅ The eyes are fixed

Every role carries the counterbored lens: dark glass, machined bezel, iris structure, black pupil,
unlit. **No sclera, no eyelashes, no eyebrows, no lid crease on any of the six.** The v3 human-eye
failure is gone across the whole set, and the face stayed constant per ROLE-001 while the chassis
changed.

### ✅ InvertMask verified in the graph

All six came back with correct alpha polarity straight out of the pipeline — border ring 0–10%
opaque, no repair needed. `fix-alpha-polarity.py --dry-run` reports "would repair 0". The v3-era
bug is closed at the source, not just patched on disk.

### ✅ The positively-named replacement panel worked

No black rectangles anywhere in the set. Naming the colour ("a *paler* off-white, slightly brighter
than the shell around it") instead of naming the difference fixed it, exactly as the eye study
predicted.

### ⚠ NEW FAILURE: a camera lens grows on any EXPOSED torso plate

**companion and security both grew a large lens assembly in the middle of the chest.** The other
four did not. The split is perfectly clean and it is not about the role:

| Role | Garment over the torso | Chest |
|---|---|---|
| companion | open smock, chest plate exposed | ⚠ **large lens** |
| security | open duty coat, chest plate exposed | ⚠ **large lens** |
| warehouse | overalls + harness, covered | ✓ clean |
| medical | apron, covered | ✓ clean |
| maintenance | coverall + tool harness, covered | ✓ clean |
| agricultural | apron, covered | ✓ clean |

**Diagnosis: the lens vocabulary fills whatever large blank plated surface is visible.** This is the
same motif-propagation mechanism as the third-optic ear in eye-study A/D, one scale up — the face
paragraph is dense with "dark glass lens / counterbored socket / machined tiers / segmented iris
ring," and at full-body framing that language spreads to the biggest available canvas. Where a
garment covers the chest there is no canvas and nothing happens.

This is an **OPT-001 break** — outcasts carry two optics, and a third on the sternum is a faction
tell violation, not a cosmetic one.

**Three fixes, in order of confidence:**
1. State the chest plate positively as blank: "the chest plate is a single unbroken panel — no
   lens, no aperture, no port, no housing." The ear clause worked; this is the same move.
2. Add to the negative: `lens on the chest, chest aperture, torso lens, lens in the abdomen`.
3. Compress the eye paragraph at full-body framing. This is the deeper fix and it **refines law
   #4**: propagate the *design*, not the *word count*. At 960×1536 the head is ~8% of frame and
   cannot resolve two machined tiers anyway — the spare capacity is what leaks. Keep the long
   version for close-ups; ship a short version ("a dark glass lens in a machined socket, bezel
   ring, iris ring, black pupil, unlit; no lashes, no brows, no sclera") for full-body.

### ⚠ Framing is inconsistent

Only **agricultural** is genuinely head-to-feet. The rest crop between mid-thigh and knee despite
"head to feet in frame" in every prompt. For a training set this is a real inconsistency — worth an
explicit "full figure, both feet visible, standing on the floor, wide shot" and a `cropped` weight
bump in the negative.
